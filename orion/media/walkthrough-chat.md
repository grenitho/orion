# Your first task

Open the chat panel (button below or the **Orion** icon in the activity
bar) and write what you want **in your own language** — Orion replies in
the language you use.

## What happens next

- Multi-part requests become a visible **todo checklist** in the chat;
  Orion keeps working until every item is done.
- The **Activity view** is the live log: *Thinking…*, *Reading folder
  structure…*, *Running tests…*
- Before anything risky runs in your terminal, an **approval modal**
  appears — nothing destructive happens silently.
- Every edit is verified with real parsers/compilers and backed up
  (`.bak`), and the final report tells you honestly what succeeded and
  what did not.

## Good first prompts

- *"Add a dark-mode toggle, write a test for it, and update the README."*
- *"Explain what this project does and draw its architecture."*
- *"Find and fix the failing test in this repo."*
